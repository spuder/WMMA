WMMA is calm, self-hosted finance for your household and every business you run. Budgets, a P&L for each business, reports worth opening, and an assistant that can read your books, all on plain-text journals that stay on your server.

Your journals, your server

Every dollar lives in plain-text hledger journals in one folder you can read, query and back up.

Exact to the cent

Money is integer cents from import to report. No floating point anywhere near your balances.

Only on your tailnet

No passwords and no public port. Tailscale tells WMMA who you are, and only people you allow get in.

Categorize

Categorizing, reduced to a swipe.

Every import runs new transactions through your rules, then through Jev’s suggestions, and leaves only the uncertain ones for you. Swipe right to accept, left to choose another category. Accept a payee three times and WMMA writes the rule for you.

  • Suggestions above 90% confidence are applied for you
  • Keyboard friendly: → accept, ← change, S save for later
  • Split a transaction across categories or businesses

Businesses

A P&L for every business you run.

Your household, an LLC, a rental: each one is an entity with categories drawn from its tax form. Move money between them with proper due-to and due-from entries, and archive one when it closes without losing a line of history.

Reports

Reports worth opening.

See where the month went in a single cash-flow picture. Then go deeper with budget against actual (with rollover), net worth over time, spending by category and a twelve-month trend, for one entity or all of them together.

  • Tap a budget line to change it
  • Houses, vehicles and investments count toward net worth

Imports

Bring it all in. Once.

Pull your full history and past budgets from YNAB, drop in QFX or CSV statements, or let SimpleFIN sync every morning. Every import is safe to run again, overlapping sources merge instead of duplicating, and the original files are kept exactly as they arrived.

  • One column profile per bank for CSV statements
  • Running balances checked against YNAB after import

AI over MCP

Ask your books anything.

WMMA serves the Model Context Protocol from the same address as the app. Connect Claude or any MCP client and it can read your reports and search transactions. With the write tools, it can also categorize, split, set budgets and record valuations. Every call is written to the audit log.

claude mcp add --transport http wmma https://wmma.<tailnet>.ts.net/mcp

Plain text

Plain text, all the way down.

Every transaction WMMA records is written to an hledger journal in your data folder, tagged with where it came from and how it was categorized. Open it in any editor or query it with hledger. If WMMA ever goes away, your books don’t.

Journals are only written by WMMA, imports are never modified, and every change from the app, the API or an assistant is appended to audit.log with a before and after.

data/journals/accounts/rewards-card.journal
2026-10-14 * Trader Joe’s  ; wmma_id:01JA8Q, source:simplefin, entity:household, cat_by:rule
    Expenses:Food:Groceries            $64.82
    Liabilities:Credit:RewardsCard    $-64.82

2026-10-15 * Figma  ; wmma_id:01JA9C, source:qfx, entity:studio, cat_by:jev, jev_conf:0.97
    Expenses:Software                  $15.00
    Liabilities:Credit:RewardsCard    $-15.00
$ hledger -f main.journal bal Expenses --tag entity=studio

Self-host

One container, on your tailnet.

  1. Configure. Copy .env.example to .env, then add a Tailscale auth key and the logins allowed in.
  2. Start. Docker Compose runs WMMA next to a Tailscale sidecar. No ports are published.
  3. Open. Visit https://wmma.<your-tailnet>.ts.net on your phone or laptop.

Data lives in ./data. Back it up like any other folder. Secrets can come from 1Password with op run.

Terminal
git clone https://github.com/spuder/WMMA.git
cd WMMA
cp .env.example .env    # TS_AUTHKEY, WMMA_ALLOWED_USERS
docker compose up -d --build
Coming from YNAB? wmma import ynab-api --include-budgets
